# Ember daily wholesale electricity prices, Germany

- `elec_daily_de.parquet`: daily wholesale electricity prices for Germany,
  the Germany rows of Ember's European wholesale electricity price data
  (daily).
- Source: Ember, European wholesale electricity price data,
  <https://ember-energy.org/data/european-wholesale-electricity-price-data/>
  (file `european_wholesale_electricity_price_data_daily.csv`). Ember states
  its hourly data comes from ENTSO-E, EMR and SEMOpx.
- Licence: CC BY 4.0. Ember: "All content is released under a Creative
  Commons Attribution Licence (CC-BY-4.0)."
- Attribution: electricity price data from Ember (https://ember-energy.org),
  licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- Built by `read_elec_daily` in the EnergyTrading project
  (`src/energy_charmers/data.py`), filtered to Germany; file dated 2026-03-10.
- The other files in this folder are not committed: several come from
  sources that do not permit redistribution.
