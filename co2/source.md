# NOAA Mauna Loa monthly mean CO2

- Source: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, <https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_mm_mlo.csv>
- Retrieved: 2026-10-27, by `Tools/fetch_noaa_co2.py` in TrainingMaterials
- Coverage: March 1958 to 2026-09
- Columns: year, month, decimal date, average (ppm), deseasonalized (ppm),
  ndays, sdev, unc. Negative ndays/sdev/unc mark interpolated months or
  Scripps-era values without that information.
- Terms: freely available; credit NOAA GML (see the file header). Data
  before May 1974 are from C. D. Keeling, Scripps Institution of
  Oceanography. December 2022 to July 2023 were measured at Maunakea
  after the Mauna Loa eruption.
- Citation: Lan, X., Tans, P. and K.W. Thoning: Trends in globally-averaged
  CO2 determined from NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory measurements,
  <https://gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/>.
